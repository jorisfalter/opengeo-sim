opengeo-sim

An open-source flight simulator that runs in a browser tab. Two of them, in fact: both take their scenery from national open geodata rather than from a pre-baked download — terrain, aerial imagery and surveyed ground geometry, streamed live, without an API key or an account.

Cab rideAmsterdam Centraal to Sloterdijk and Lelylaan, over PDOK terrain and 8 cm aerial photography. FlightA light jet you fly yourself, on JSBSim. Start on the runway at Chambéry, on the slope at Courchevel, or anywhere in the world.
