opengeo-sim

Two browser simulators that take their scenery from national open geodata rather than from a pre-baked download: terrain, aerial imagery and surveyed ground geometry, streamed live, without an API key.

Cab rideAmsterdam Centraal to Sloterdijk and Lelylaan, over PDOK terrain and 8 cm aerial photography. FlightA light jet you fly yourself, on JSBSim. Start on the runway at Chambéry, on the slope at Courchevel, or anywhere in the world.
